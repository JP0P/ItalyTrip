Italy Trip Overview (May 13–27, 2026)

Route: Rome → Amalfi Coast → Florence → Tuscany → Cinque Terre → Venice
Nights: 2 Rome / 4 Amalfi / 2 Florence / 3 Tuscany / 2 Cinque Terre / 1 Venice

⸻

Rome (May 13–15) — Where to Stay

Recommended areas:
• Trastevere (lively evenings, walkable dining)
• Pantheon / Campo de’ Fiori (central, flat, easy taxis)

Rome Highlights

May 14:
	•	Morning Vatican Museums, Sistine Chapel, St. Peter’s Basilica (guided)
	•	Afternoon: Colosseum & Forum OR Capitoline Hill
	•	Evening in Trastevere

Train: Rome → Naples (May 15)
	•	Depart Roma Termini at 10:20 CET
	•	Frecciarossa 8335 (1h13m)
	•	Arrive Napoli Centrale at 11:33 CET

⸻

Amalfi Coast (May 15–19) — Where to Stay

Recommended bases:
• Amalfi town (best ferry access, flatter walking)
• Ravello (quieter, dramatic views, calmer pace)

Amalfi Highlights
	•	Capri day trip
	•	Half-day cooking class in Amalfi
	•	One rest day
	•	One coastal town/ferry-hopping day

Train: Naples → Florence (May 19)
	•	Depart Napoli Centrale at 12:10 CET
	•	Frecciarossa 9422 (3h01m)
	•	Arrive Firenze Santa Maria Novella at 15:11 CET

⸻

Florence (May 19–21) — Where to Stay

Recommended areas:
• Historic Center near Duomo or Santa Croce
• Oltrarno (local feel, quieter nights)

Florence Highlights
	•	Duomo exterior
	•	ONE museum (Accademia or Uffizi)
	•	Sunset at Piazzale Michelangelo

⸻

Tuscany Countryside (May 21–24) — Where to Stay

Recommended area:
• Val d’Orcia (Pienza, Montepulciano vicinity)
Ideal for Vespa riding and hot air balloon launches.

Tuscany Highlights
	•	Vespa day (Pienza/Montepulciano)
	•	Sunrise hot air balloon (weather permitting)
	•	Hill towns and winery lunches

Car Return & Transfer (Sunday, May 24)
	•	Drive Val d’Orcia → Pisa Airport (2.5–3 hrs)
	•	Return car at Pisa Airport (open Sundays)
	•	Train Pisa Airport → La Spezia Centrale (~1 hr), then local train into Cinque Terre village

⸻

Cinque Terre (May 24–26) — Where to Stay

Recommended villages:
• Vernazza (most iconic views)
• Monterosso al Mare (easiest walking, beaches)

Cinque Terre Highlights
	•	Village hopping by train
	•	Sunset in Manarola
	•	Relaxed coastal mornings

⸻

Venice (May 26–27) — Where to Stay

Recommended neighborhoods:
• Dorsoduro (calmer, scenic, close to gondola routes)
• Cannaregio (local vibe, excellent dining)

Venice Highlights
	•	May 26 evening: 8:20pm gondola ride booked
	•	Cicchetti crawl and evening canal wandering
